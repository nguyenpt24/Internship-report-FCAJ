# Summary Report: “FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week”

### General Information

* **Event Name:** FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week
* **Date & Time:** 09:00, July 25, 2026
* **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Role:** Attendee

### Content and Main Activities

Winning teams from the Agentic AI competition presented their products, shared their design methodologies, and discussed their practical journey throughout the hackathon.

### Outcomes and Value Gained

#### System Design Mindset
Teams emphasized the importance of clearly understanding data workflows and choosing the appropriate AWS services for cost optimization (e.g., using Amazon Bedrock or AWS Fargate). This insight is directly applicable to clarifying the real-time messaging workflow in the Serverless Chat Workshop project.

#### Optimization Skills
Teams shared approaches to addressing latency challenges and maintaining cost effectiveness. These are critical factors when implementing real-time chat applications on serverless architecture (integrating API Gateway WebSocket, AWS Lambda, and DynamoDB).

#### Teamwork Experience
Clear role assignment (Backend, Frontend, Cloud Architect, Pitching) was key to completing projects on schedule. For the Serverless Chat Workshop, seamlessly integrating AWS Amplify (for hosting the dashboard) with state/messaging management services enables building a professional Proof of Concept (POC).

#### Continuous Learning and Troubleshooting
Participating in the event provided an opportunity to observe real-world challenges faced by competing teams, thereby sharpening AWS console troubleshooting capabilities.
