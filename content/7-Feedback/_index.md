# Personal Experience Sharing & Feedback

---

### Acknowledgments

First and foremost, I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the organizing team of the **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited**, and our dedicated Mentors for supporting me throughout the 9-week internship period (from June 08, 2026 to August 04, 2026).

This internship has not only helped me solidify foundational concepts learned at Saigon University but also provided a valuable opportunity to work with modern AWS Cloud technologies and build a live application on AWS infrastructure.

---

### 1. Internship Experience Evaluation

#### Learning & Work Environment
The FCAJ program environment is open, professional, and supportive. Group discussions and lab troubleshooting questions are handled actively by mentors and peers alike.

#### Support from Mentors & Program Administration
Mentors provided thorough guidance, encouraging analytical problem-solving rather than just handing out answers. The administrative team efficiently supported AWS account resources, documentation, and student inquiries.

#### Academic Relevance
The internship content closely aligns with Information Technology major subjects while extending into modern cloud disciplines such as Serverless Programming, Event-Driven Architecture, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and Cloud Security.

#### Skill Development Opportunities
Over the 9 weeks, I expanded my hands-on experience with AWS CLI/Console, improved my technical documentation skills, and enhanced my time management and teamwork abilities.

---

### 2. Personal Insights & Reflections

- **Most Satisfying Aspect**: Successfully building and deploying the **Serverless Real-Time Chat Application** (integrating API Gateway WebSocket, AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, CloudFront, Cognito, and CloudWatch). Seeing a live application operate with low response latency was the highlight of my internship.
- **Recommending to Peers**: I would strongly recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to junior students at my university, as it offers a structured, practical, and highly beneficial learning path for cloud technology careers.

---

### 3. Suggestions & Future Goals

- **Certification Sharing Sessions**: It would be great if the program could include 1 or 2 webinars sharing preparation strategies for AWS certifications (such as the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate) for students planning to take certification exams after the internship.
- **Future Engagement**: I look forward to staying connected with the AWS Study Group community and participating in future AWS Vietnam technical events.
